About this pilot

Mercury Plus is an early public, non-commercial browser pilot for exploring Ho Chi Minh City. At this stage, only the OpenStreetMap base map is connected. Other city datasets are being checked for source quality, access conditions and licence requirements.

This interface is experimental. It is not an emergency-response service and must not be used as the sole basis for safety-critical decisions.
